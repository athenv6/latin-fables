De gallo et iaspide

Dum rigido fodit ore fimum, dum queritat escam, tunc stupet inventa iaspide gallus. Ayt: ▲

Meaning: The fable contrasts foolishness (cock in dung) with wisdom (jasper).

“Res vili preciosa loco varique decoris, hac in sorde iaces; nil michi messis habes. Si tibi nunc esset qui debet esse repertor... Plus amo cara minus.”
